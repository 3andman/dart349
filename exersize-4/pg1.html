A Day Without Water

A short HTML narrative about what happens when something essential disappears.

7:12 AM

You turn the tap. Nothing comes out. The city alert arrives five minutes later.

12:34 PM

Bottled water is gone. Cafés are closed. Schools send students home early.

4:56 PM

People line up at a community center. Some leave empty-handed.

9:18 PM

The water will be back tomorrow. For some people, that’s soon enough. For others, it isn’t.

This isn’t fiction.

Climate change, infrastructure neglect, and inequality decide who goes without first.
